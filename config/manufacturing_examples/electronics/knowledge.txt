Synthetic table accounting
For each product separately, home May has material/labor/overhead unit costs 10/4/6 CNY/piece, unit cost 20 CNY/piece, output 100 piece and total 2000 CNY. Home June uses 12/5/6, unit cost 23, output 120 and total 2760. Peer May uses 9/4/5, unit cost 18, output 90 and total 1620. Peer June uses 10/4/5, unit cost 19, output 110 and total 2090. Every budget row uses 10/4/6, unit cost 20, output 100 and total 2000. Both products deliberately repeat the same base costs; no per-product cost or output is doubled.
Each actual identity has two declared component rows in the materials table. Each row carries half the material unit cost, and its amount equals that unit cost times product output. The first component row supplies synthetic source quantity equal numerically to output, quantity unit piece and unit price equal to half the material unit cost. The second leaves quantity, quantity_unit and unit_price empty. Missing actual quantities/prices stay unknown and must not be filled from the reference BOM or a reference price. Runtime source_observed is a provenance label for these supplied synthetic cells, not evidence of real-world measurement.
Each actual identity has one labor row: hours equal numerically to output, hourly_rate equals labor unit cost in CNY/hour, and amount equals hours times hourly_rate. Headcount is 5 and working_days is 20; these are synthetic observations, not a staffing schedule or assumptions for calculating hours.
Each identity has SIMULATION Energy and SIMULATION Maintenance overhead categories; each carries half the overhead unit cost with an amount equal to unit cost times output. No real maintenance or energy event is asserted.

Two months and synthetic factories do not prove production comparability, efficiency, market prices, seasonality or forecasts. Only explicit piece-to-piece and CNY-to-CNY identity conversions are configured; no panel, reel, package, area-to-count or mass-to-count conversion is implied.
